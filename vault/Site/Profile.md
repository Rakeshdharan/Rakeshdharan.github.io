## For everyone

I'm a robotics engineer finishing my master's in Automation and Robotics at TU Dortmund. I teach robots new skills — by letting them practise millions of times in simulation, or by showing them examples — and I write the control software that keeps them precise and safe in the real world.

Before robotics research, I spent two years at Polaris testing the software inside vehicle control units. That's where I learned to care about reliability. Today I'm an intern at Porsche Engineering, working on humanoid, four-legged and wheeled robots for industrial use.

## For engineers

M.Sc. Automation & Robotics at TU Dortmund. I work across the robotics stack: reinforcement learning (PPO, SAC, DDPG) and imitation learning (BC, DAgger, ACT / SmolVLA / X-VLA policies with LeRobot), nonlinear MPC in CasADi, motion planning (MoveIt, RRT / PRM / TEB), and ROS 1/2 integration in Docker.

Currently a Robotics / Physical AI intern at Porsche Engineering — sim-to-sim and sim-to-real for humanoid, quadruped and wheeled platforms. Previously 2+ years of hardware-in-the-loop validation of vehicle ECUs at Polaris (UDS, J1939 over CAN; NI VeriStand, dSPACE ControlDesk).

## Robot learning
> Teaching robots skills through practice in simulation or by learning from demonstrations.

- PPO, SAC, DDPG, SARSA
- Behaviour cloning, DAgger
- ACT, SmolVLA, X-VLA (LeRobot)
- PyTorch, Gymnasium

## Control
> The maths that keeps a robot on its planned path, even when the world pushes back.

- Nonlinear & robust MPC
- CasADi
- PID, trajectory tracking
- System modelling

## Planning & navigation
> Working out where to go and how to get there without hitting anything.

- RRT, PRM, TEB
- MoveIt, task constructors
- SLAM

## Robot software
> The plumbing that connects sensors, brains and motors into one working system.

- ROS 1 / ROS 2
- C++, Python, C, Bash
- Docker, Linux, Git
- Jira, Confluence

## Simulation
> Virtual worlds where robots can train and fail safely before touching real hardware.

- Isaac Sim, Isaac Lab, Isaac Gym
- MuJoCo, Newton
- robosuite, ManiSkill3, Genesis
- Gazebo, RViz, PyBullet

## Perception
> Helping robots see and make sense of camera images.

- OpenCV
- YOLO / Ultralytics
- AprilTags, colour segmentation

## Hardware I've worked with
> Real robots and boards I've programmed.

- Unitree G1, Unitree Go2, Booster T1
- UR5, UR10, UR15, SO-101
- TurtleBot3
- Raspberry Pi, Jetson, Arduino

## Embedded & testing
> Making sure the software inside machines works before it ships.

- Hardware-in-the-loop (HIL)
- NI VeriStand, dSPACE ControlDesk
- CAN, UDS, J1939
- CANalyzer, CANoe, CAPL
